01 / About the project

What is Smart Karachi?

Karachi is a busy and important city of Pakistan. Smart Karachi is a student project that shares ideas for improving daily life through planning, community teamwork and modern technology.

A city full of potential

A smart city uses resources wisely and makes public services easier to access. Small positive actions by citizens can also make a big difference.

Our main goals

  • Improve public transport and road safety
  • Keep streets and public places clean
  • Protect trees, parks and water
  • Use technology to support people
02 / Move better

Smart transportation

Good transport helps people reach schools, workplaces, hospitals and other places more easily.

🚌

Better public buses

Reliable, comfortable buses can make public transport a useful choice for more people.

🚦

Smart traffic signals

Well-managed traffic signals and clear road signs can help traffic move more smoothly.

🚶

Safer footpaths

Clean footpaths and safe crossings make walking easier for children and families.

03 / Care for nature

Clean and green Karachi

A healthy city needs clean surroundings, responsible waste disposal and green spaces.

🌳

Plant more trees

Planting and caring for trees can provide shade and make neighbourhoods greener.

♻️

Reduce waste

Use bins, separate recyclable materials where possible and avoid littering.

💧

Save water

Repair leaks, turn off taps when not needed and use water carefully.

04 / Helpful innovation

Technology for everyone

Technology can make information and public services easier to reach. It should be simple, safe and useful for all citizens.

📱

Digital information

Online updates can help people find useful public information and services.

🏫

Smart education

Computers, internet access and digital learning tools can support students and teachers.

🏥

Better healthcare

Accessible health information and well-equipped hospitals can support healthier communities.

05 / Everyone can help

How can we make it happen?

A smarter city is built step by step. Here are four simple actions that can help.

01

Keep it clean

Put rubbish in bins and never throw waste on roads.

02

Save resources

Save electricity and water at home and school.

03

Grow green

Look after plants and take part in tree-planting activities.

04

Work together

Share helpful ideas and respect public spaces.
